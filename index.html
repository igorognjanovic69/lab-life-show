About me

Dr. Nevena Jeremić

Education & research consulting · Associate Professor · teaching you how to design and publish better science.

Nevena Jeremić is an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Medical Sciences, University of Kragujevac, where she has worked full-time since 2013 in the field of pharmaceutical chemistry.

She earned her degree in Pharmacy in 2012 and completed her doctorate in Clinical and Experimental Pharmacology in 2014, both at the University of Kragujevac. From 2016 to 2018 she was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Physiology, School of Medicine, University of Louisville, Kentucky, USA.

Her work bridges experimental pharmacology and physiology, with a focus on cardiovascular and metabolic disease, oxidative stress, and natural products. Alongside her research, she mentors scientists on study design, scientific writing, and publishing.
